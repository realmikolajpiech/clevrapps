Forkday / Account and data

Delete your Forkday account

Request deletion from the app or by email.

How to request deletion

In Forkday, open You → Account and data → Request account deletion. Your email app will prepare a request containing your anonymous Forkday account ID. Send that email to mikolaj@clevrapps.com.

If you cannot open Forkday, email the same address with the subject “Delete my Forkday account” and include your account ID if you saved it. We may ask for information needed to identify your account before deleting it. We will confirm when the request has been handled.

What is deleted

We delete the Forkday account and associated cloud data we control, including meal preferences, household details, pantry and shopping lists, recipes, uploaded food photos or voice requests still stored by us, plans, and cooking history. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Local data remains on your device until you remove the app. Subscription purchase and payment records are managed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat and may be retained as required for accounting, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Deleting a Forkday account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it separately in your App Store or Google Play account.

Questions

For a copy of your data or to request deletion of specific data without deleting your account, contact mikolaj@clevrapps.com. See the Clevr Apps privacy policy for more detail.
