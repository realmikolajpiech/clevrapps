Forkday / Account and data

Delete your Forkday account

Delete directly in the app, or contact us if you no longer have access.

How to delete your account

In Forkday, open You → Account and data → Delete account and data. Read the consequences, then tap Permanently delete my account. The app deletes your account and cloud data and starts a fresh account on this device.

If you cannot open Forkday, email mikolaj@clevrapps.com with the subject “Delete my Forkday account” and include your account ID if you saved it. We may ask for information needed to identify the account. We aim to complete verified email requests within 30 days and will confirm when handled.

What is deleted

We delete the Forkday account and associated cloud data we control, including meal preferences, household details, pantry and shopping lists, recipes, uploaded food photos or voice requests still stored by us, plans, and cooking history. In-app deletion also clears Forkday data stored on that device.

Subscription purchase and payment records are managed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat and may be retained as required for accounting, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Deleting a Forkday account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it separately in your App Store or Google Play account.

Questions

For a copy of your data or to request deletion of specific data without deleting your account, contact mikolaj@clevrapps.com. See the Clevr Apps privacy policy for more detail.
