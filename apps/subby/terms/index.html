Using Subby

Subby helps you track recurring bills, memberships and free trials. It uses the records you enter to estimate spending and schedule reminders. Check prices, renewal dates and cancellation deadlines directly with the provider you pay. Reminder delivery depends on your device permissions and settings, so check time-sensitive deadlines separately.

To change or cancel a service you track in Subby, contact that service provider. Editing or removing a record in Subby affects only your tracking list.

You may use Subby for lawful purposes on compatible devices you own or control, subject to the applicable app store license. Use only information you have permission to use and respect the rights of others.

Subby Pro purchases

Subby is available for free with limits. Optional Subby Pro purchases unlock features such as unlimited tracked subscriptions and multiple profiles. Available plans can vary by store and region. The purchase screen shows the price, billing period and any trial or introductory offer for the plan you select. Google Play or the App Store handles billing through the account you use to confirm the purchase.

Auto-renewing plans renew and charge your store account for each new billing period unless you cancel before the renewal deadline shown by the store. If your plan starts with a free trial, it converts to a paid subscription at the end of the trial unless you cancel before the store's deadline. An available lifetime option is a one-time purchase with no recurring charge.

Manage, cancel or restore a purchase

After cancellation, Pro access generally continues until the end of the current paid period, subject to the store's terms and applicable law. Cancel through the store even if you uninstall Subby. To recover an eligible purchase, use Restore Purchases in Subby while signed in to the original store account.
