Solvee

iOS & Android · Acquired in 2026

Part of the Clevr Apps archive

From a question to an explanation.

Solvee helped students scan homework and work through problems step by step. It reached more than 25,000 downloads before being acquired in February 2026.

How it worked

  • Camera-first input

    Point the camera at an exercise instead of spending time typing mathematical notation.

  • Detailed explanations

    Move beyond a final answer with reasoning designed to show how a problem can be approached.

  • Built, grown and acquired

    Solvee reached 25,000+ downloads and became Clevr Apps’ first product acquisition in February 2026.
