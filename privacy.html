01 Information Processed and Where It Comes From

Information you choose to provide

  • Correspondence: your name, email address, message, attachments, and ordinary email metadata when you contact me.
  • Accounts, where offered: email address, display name, service-generated user identifier, authentication credentials handled by the authentication provider, and information returned by Apple or Google if you choose their sign-in service.
  • Subby: the subscription records you enter, including service names, prices, currency, billing dates, categories, profile names, and optional income information. Current versions store these records on your device. Older versions may have account or synchronized records held in Supabase.
  • Optional Subby bank connection: if you use this feature, Enable Banking and the bank you authorize provide account and transaction information needed to find recurring payments. Subby's server processes those records; unmatched merchant descriptions, amounts, and currencies can be sent to xAI to identify the subscription, including through web search. You can add subscriptions manually instead.
  • Doso: medication names, dosage and timing instructions, schedules, dose history, inventory, profiles, health journal entries, and preferences entered on your device. Optional Connected Family sends shared profiles, medication plans, dose history, account and household membership information, invitations, and notification settings to Supabase and makes shared records accessible to authorized household members. Optional AI requests can include medication names, text or dictated transcripts, questions, and optional age or biological sex; they pass through Doso's server to xAI. The Doso notice describes these choices in more detail.
  • Trailo: account information, trip plans, planning messages, uploaded travel documents, traveler details, invitations, and collaboration or booking information that you choose to submit. Relevant messages and selected trip information are processed by AI services as described in the Trailo notice.
  • User-provided images in the Meant app (which may contain faces or other personal depictions) – these are stored exclusively locally on your device and never uploaded to my servers

Purchases, technical information, and notifications

The apps offer subscriptions. Apple or Google handles store payments; apps using RevenueCat send an app-specific purchaser identifier, product identifiers, purchase receipts or tokens, transaction and renewal status, and necessary device or app information to validate, restore, and manage access. I do not receive your full payment-card number. Where a web payment is offered through Stripe, Stripe handles the payment details and returns the information needed to confirm the purchase and meet billing obligations.

Connections to the website and online app features expose technical information such as IP address, request time, browser or app version, platform, response status, and error information to the relevant server and infrastructure providers. Purchase and authentication providers also supply transaction and account status. Notification services process a device token and the delivery information needed for an enabled notification feature. Local reminders can operate on the device without a remote push token.

Whether information is required

Providing information is voluntary unless a particular legal obligation applies. Without required account information, an account cannot be created; without transaction verification, paid access cannot be activated or restored; without a submitted AI request, that AI feature cannot provide an answer. You can decline optional microphone, location, photo, and notification access, although the associated feature will be unavailable. Information about another person should be supplied only where you have authority and an appropriate legal basis to do so.

03 Legal Bases

  • Requested services and purchases — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR: processing objectively necessary to provide an account, requested online feature, synchronization, subscription entitlement, or payment-related service under the applicable contract.
  • Support and security — Article 6(1)(f): the legitimate interests of answering enquiries, maintaining reliable services, investigating errors, preventing abuse and fraud, and establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims. These interests must be balanced against users' rights, particularly where children are concerned.
  • Legal obligations — Article 6(1)(c): processing required by applicable tax, accounting, consumer, or privacy law.
  • Optional processing requiring consent — Article 6(1)(a): the particular purpose described when consent is requested. A device permission controls technical access; it is not permission for unrelated processing.

Health information: an Article 6 basis alone does not permit processing of special-category health data. Optional Doso AI processing of health information relies on explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a), requested separately before transmission. You can decline that feature or withdraw consent in Doso's AI settings. Sharing another person's health information requires their applicable consent or another valid legal authority; a purchase or subscription does not itself provide that authority.

Withdrawing consent stops the processing that depends on it without making earlier processing unlawful. You can contact mikolaj@clevrapps.com about withdrawal or an objection. Necessary service processing is not treated as optional consent simply because the feature is accessed through an app.

05 Image Data and Face Data in the Meant App

In the Meant app, you may provide images (via camera or photo library) that contain human faces for AI-based editing and generation. These images constitute pixel data and may include sensitive face depictions.

Collection and Storage: Images are stored exclusively locally on your device in the app’s private storage. I do not collect, upload, or store any images on my servers or in any database.

Use: Images are processed locally and sent to Google’s Gemini API solely to perform the user-requested AI image generation or editing. The app performs no facial recognition, biometric extraction, identification, tracking, or profiling.

Disclosure and Sharing: Images are shared only with Google LLC (as the Gemini API provider) for temporary processing. The Meant app uses the paid tier of the Gemini API, where your inputs (including images) and outputs are not used to train or improve Google’s models.

Retention: Images are retained only on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Google processes images transiently and retains them only as necessary for API service provision (typically briefly; no long-term storage for training).

For full details on Google’s data handling, please see the Gemini API Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

06 Retention and Deletion

  • Local information: retained until you delete the relevant records, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it, subject to the platform's storage and backup behavior. Exported files and copies in your own backups must be managed separately.
  • Accounts and online records: retained while needed to provide the requested account or feature, until you delete the relevant data or account, subject to necessary legal exceptions. Shared records may remain for other authorized participants where there is a continuing lawful reason.
  • Support correspondence: retained while handling the enquiry and, where necessary afterward, to document its resolution or address a related dispute. Further retention must be limited to the applicable obligation or claim period.
  • Purchase and accounting records: retained for the applicable statutory tax and accounting periods and any necessary refund, fraud, or dispute handling. Payment providers may retain records independently under their own obligations.
  • Technical logs: retained for the period needed to operate, secure, and diagnose the relevant service, taking account of the provider's configured log-retention period. Incident records may need to be retained until the incident or associated claim is resolved.
  • Backups: deleted records can remain in restricted backups until those backups expire or are replaced in the applicable backup cycle. They must not be restored to ordinary use without applying the deletion again.
  • AI and bank-service requests: providers can retain request information under their applicable service, abuse-prevention, and legal-retention terms. A local storage feature does not mean information sent to a provider is never retained there.

Where an app offers account deletion, use its account controls or contact mikolaj@clevrapps.com to request deletion from outside the app. Identify the app and the account or record concerned. I may request proportionate information to verify your authority; do not send passwords. I will explain any specific legal reason for retaining information after a deletion request.

Subscription cancellation is separate from data or account deletion. Manage an Apple or Google subscription in the relevant store's subscription settings. Uninstalling an app or deleting its account does not automatically cancel a store subscription.

Where GDPR applies, you can request access to your personal information, a copy, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Send requests to mikolaj@clevrapps.com. I respond without undue delay and normally within one month. Where another applicable privacy law gives you additional rights, you can exercise them using the same contact address.

I use measures appropriate to the service and risk, including encrypted transport for online connections, access restrictions, and the security facilities of the relevant providers. Security differs between local files, account storage, shared records, and information sent to an external service. No electronic transmission or storage method guarantees complete security.
